Eleven years on the same stretch of Fleurieu grass

Paringa Park View has run dairy cattle on this stretch of South Australian coastline since 2015. No feedlots, no shortcuts — just deep-rooted perennial pasture, mineral-rich soil, and cows that walk further than most people drive in a day. What comes off the farm is what we'd feed our own kids.

We're a small operation by choice. Every drop is vat-pasteurised on-site, never homogenised, and on your table within 48 hours of milking.
