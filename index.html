Three generations on the same stretch of Fleurieu grass

Paringa Park View has run dairy cattle on this stretch of South Australian coastline since 1962. No feedlots, no shortcuts — just deep-rooted perennial pasture, mineral-rich soil, and cows that walk further than most people drive in a day. What comes off the farm is what we'd feed our own kids.

We're a small operation by choice. Every drop is vat-pasteurised on-site, never homogenised, and on your table within 48 hours of milking.

Farmed the way it should always have been

Regenerative grazing isn't a label here — it's how the paddocks have always been rested, rotated and read before every herd move.

Rotational Grazing

Paddocks rest up to 30 days between grazes, letting root systems rebuild.

No Added Hormones

Our herd grows at its own pace, on grass, water and time.
