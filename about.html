Get In Touch

Looking after the land means looking after everyone it feeds

Paringa Park View is a family dairy on South Australia's Fleurieu Peninsula, connecting fresh milk, cream and butter to the cafes and households around us.

What we stand for

Four simple promises

Grass first

Our cows live on deep-rooted pasture, rotated and rested so the paddock keeps its strength.

Small by choice

We stay small so we can do things slowly and properly, from the morning milking to the last bottle.

Glass, not waste

Returnable glass bottles keep the cream line intact and the packaging out of landfill.

Local, always

Fresh milk should travel a short way. We supply the Fleurieu and the neighbours around it.

Our story

Eleven years on the same stretch of Fleurieu grass

  1. 2015

    It started with the paddock

    Paringa Park View began with a simple idea: let good pasture do most of the work, and treat the cows like part of the family.

  2. Every day

    Twice a day, every day

    Milking happens at first light and again in the afternoon. The routine has barely changed. It works, so we keep it.

  3. Today

    Still family, still local

    Eleven years on, the same people are milking, bottling and delivering. We know the farms, the cafes and the families we supply.
